The problem

Your next lost booking may never show up in your analytics.

Long before a traveler reaches your booking engine, they encounter your hotel across a fragmented digital journey — and much of that journey is invisible in your own reporting.

→Google — search results, maps and hotel packs shaped by third parties.
→OTAs — capturing demand, the transaction and the guest relationship.
→AI assistants — surfacing (or omitting) your property for real traveler questions.
→Review platforms — where reputation quietly shapes the shortlist.
→Travel sites — "best of" lists and aggregators that route demand elsewhere.
→Competing hotels — appearing alongside you for the same traveler intent.

Innavisio examines that journey — from discovery to direct booking — to show where demand is being intercepted, and where the direct path itself gives travelers too little reason to book with you.
